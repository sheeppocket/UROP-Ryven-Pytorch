# DeepWeeds demo photos

These 175 field photos are from Alex Olsen and colleagues’ *DeepWeeds: A Multiclass Weed Species Image Dataset for Deep Learning* (2019). The original JPEG files and filenames are preserved. The photos are licensed under CC BY 4.0. [Dataset and labels](https://github.com/AlexOlsen/DeepWeeds) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-38343-3) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Each of the five species has 25 photos in `train` and 10 in `test`. The folder names come from the authors’ labels. The subset draws from the published fold 0 training and test lists and keeps capture dates separate between the two splits. The test score describes this small teaching subset.
